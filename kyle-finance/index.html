Kyle Finance

A personal budgeting app for macOS.

Kyle Finance is a small Mac app I built for my own household. Once a week it gathers our recent bank and credit card transactions, suggests a category for each one, and lets me review them. Once a month it does the same for account balances. Only after I approve the changes does it write them to my own budget spreadsheets in Google Sheets.

What it does with Google

The app uses the Google Sheets API to read and update two spreadsheets that I own: a monthly spending sheet and a net-worth sheet. It doesn't open any other files, and it doesn't use Gmail, Drive, Contacts or any other Google service.

Who can use it

Kyle Finance is a personal tool. It isn't offered to the public, and only my own Google account signs in to it.
